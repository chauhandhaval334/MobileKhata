Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 05, 2026

Welcome to MobileKhata! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your data when you use the MobileKhata mobile application (the "App") and its associated website services.

MobileKhata is developed and operated by Dhwix ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your business details, inventory lists, customer ledgers, and transactions. By using the Service, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

To provide a robust ledger, IMEI inventory tracking, and billing features, MobileKhata accesses the following details:

  • Business Details: Shop Name, GSTIN, Shop Address, Owner Name, District, and contact info to customize your ledger and PDF invoices.
  • Transaction Data: Details of Sales, Purchases, Repairs, Customer Names, Mobile Numbers, IMEI numbers, and device specifications entered in the ledger.
  • Document Images: Scans of customer ID proofs, purchase bills, or device photos linked to transaction records.

2. Permissions We Request and Why

To operate ledger features seamlessly on Android, we request specific device permissions. We do not sell or abuse your personal/business data:

📷 Camera Access (CAMERA)
Required to scan barcodes and IMEI numbers on devices to speed up inventory search and billing.
📁 Storage & Media Access (READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)
Required to generate, export, save, and share professional PDF invoice bills and import ledger backups or upload transaction documents.
🌐 Network Access & Internet (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)
Required to securely back up your ledger database, sync details with our cloud server in real time, and fetch active plans.
🔔 Notification Access (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)
Used to alert you about system sync updates, backup completions, and repair status alerts.
💳 Google Play Billing (BILLING)
Allows you to securely purchase premium subscription plans directly inside the app using Google Pay.

3. Data Synchronization & Backup Security

All data entered in the MobileKhata app is saved securely in a local database (Room DB) on your device. When connected to the internet, transaction logs are securely synchronized with our cloud backend to ensure you never lose your data. We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (SSL/TLS) for data transmission and store synced data securely.

4. Third-Party Services We Use

We work with trusted third-party providers to enhance app performance, manage payments, and run analytics:

  • Google Play Billing: Securely processes all premium subscriptions. We do not store or process your credit/debit card details.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Used to track anonymous usage statistics and identify system bugs/crashes to improve user experience.
  • Room Database: Local Android SQLite library to store ledger records offline on your phone safely.

5. In-App Purchases and Payment Terms

MobileKhata offers optional premium plans. When you purchase a subscription, payments are processed and managed entirely by Google Play In-App Payments in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Your subscription will automatically renew at the end of each billing period unless auto-renew is disabled in your Google Account Settings at least 24 hours before renewal.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app and services are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted on this page and will become effective immediately upon publication.
