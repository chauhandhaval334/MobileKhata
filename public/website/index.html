Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Is my business data secure?

Absolutely. Your ledger database is fully encrypted and synced to cloud nodes in real-time. Only you can access your account using phone OTP validation.

Can I run MobileKhata offline?

Yes. You can add entries, view inventories, and look up customers without internet connection. Any changes you make will sync automatically once connection is restored.

How do I upgrade to the premium subscription?

Load the app, go to Settings > Plan & Subscription, choose your plan, and complete the payment using Google Play Billing. Premium is activated immediately upon payment verification.

What if I change my phone?

Just download the MobileKhata app on your new phone, enter your registered mobile number, and enter the verification OTP. All your transactions, stock records, and ledger balances will be synchronized automatically.
